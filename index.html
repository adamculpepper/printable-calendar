About

A calendar generator that prints straight from the browser. It is a static page with no build step and no framework, and it loads nothing from anywhere else. Whatever you set up here is in the address bar, so a configuration is a link you can keep or send on.

Holiday dates come from holidate, an MIT-licensed library by Zachary Gomez. It is installed from npm and bundled into this page rather than fetched at runtime, and its license travels with it in vendor/holidate. It covers United States holidays, which is why the Holidays control is US only. Easter and the dates derived from it are recalculated here so they stay right outside the years the library's lookup table was built for.
